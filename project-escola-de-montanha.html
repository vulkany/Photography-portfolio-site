Escola de Montanha  ·  2023–present

Founded in 2019 in Serra da Freita, Arouca — a community built around the vertical world.

This non-profit association focuses on training practitioners and instructors in climbing, mountaineering, canyoning and alpinism. With over 250 members, since 2023 photographic documentation has been integral to the association's work — capturing technical disciplines alongside shared effort, interpersonal trust, and the focused atmosphere of mountain activities.
