How it works
01

Brief

Understanding the project, the brand, the audience and the intended outcome. This is where the visual direction is defined.

02

Pre-Production

Location scouting, casting, moodboards, scheduling and technical preparation. Every detail is resolved before the first frame is shot.

03

Production

The shoot or film day. Focused, efficient and collaborative — with enough flexibility to capture what wasn't planned but needed to exist.

04

Post & Delivery

Editing, retouching and colour grading. Delivered in agreed formats with full usage rights. The project is only finished when the client is satisfied.
