Documentary Film

Feira do Fumeiro
Montalegre

Montalegre  ·  2021

The 30th edition of this beloved annual tradition in Portugal's Barroso region, reimagined as a fully online experience.

The fair celebrates the ancient craft of smoke-curing meats — from chouriço to presunto — passed down through generations. Produced entirely during pandemic restrictions, this documentary brought the sights and smells of Montalegre's most iconic event to audiences across Portugal and beyond.
