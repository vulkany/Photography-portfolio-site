Photography

Climbing

Personal  ·  Portugal & Spain

A road trip at dusk, a body against red rock bathed in the last light of the day.

Crampons biting into frozen waterfalls, boulders tackled well past dark under pine trees and headlamps. A photographic documentary of climbers across Portugal and Spain — limestone crags, snow-covered mountains, and the quiet intensity of people who climb not for fame, but because the rock demands it.
